# Discover

Read HARNESS.md, the profile, raw recent feedback, hypotheses and approved scoped rubric. Create a neutral 3–4 option round for the chosen domain/context. In an initial round, span families. Later choose two plausible fits, one adjacent direction and one wildcard, or a 2-option matched test. Keep selection rationale private until after voting.

Choose the next comparison that could distinguish competing hypotheses. State the changed variable internally and log confounds; do not call cross-brand choices causal experiments. Avoid repeating an unresolved round indefinitely; unreviewed references remain available.

Use `templates/round.json`. For each reference, record title, inspectable content, source kind, source URL if external, observation date, inspection status and rights note. Never fabricate a URL or observation. Original writing exercises are valid and must say original. Do not access paywalls or private sources without permission. Present actual examples together and ask: “Which attracts you most and least? Ties, none and partial likes are welcome.” Wait for feedback before interpretation.
