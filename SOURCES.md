# Sources and credits

This kit organizes a workflow developed through iterative choices and corrections. It is not an official product of the organizations linked below.

## Direct inspiration

- [Chase AI: How to Design Front-End Websites with GPT Astra](https://www.chaseai.io/blog/gpt-astra-front-end-design): collecting references in a taste vault, organizing design families, and narrowing variations. This repo extends that idea into explicit positive/negative evidence, provisional hypotheses, corrections, approval and general judgement workflows. Product/version claims in that article are not dependencies of this kit.
- **@404:** credited by the project author as an inspiration. A canonical post URL and unambiguous identity were not supplied. Retain the attribution without inventing a link or attributing specific claims.

## Related ideas, not evidence that this kit is validated

- [Poddar et al., Personalizing Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback with Variational Preference Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10075): personalized/heterogeneous preference modeling.
- [Chakraborty et al., MaxMin-RLHF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08925): limitations of aggregating diverse preferences into a single reward model.
- [Doshi and Hauser, Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11244532/): findings from a short-story experiment; not a universal explanation of generic design.
- [Pierre Bourdieu, Distinction](https://www.routledge.com/Distinction-A-Social-Critique-of-the-Judgement-of-Taste/Bourdieu/p/book/9780415567886): taste and social distinction.
- [Paul Graham, Taste for Makers](https://paulgraham.com/taste.html): an essay about developing judgement through practice.

Preference diversity does not imply preferences cannot be learned in weights. External context is the engineering choice here. “Encoding/decoding” names the workflow of articulation and application, not a tested computational theory of taste.

## Candidate pools

[Refero](https://refero.design/) ([how it works](https://refero.design/how-it-works)), [Land-book](https://land-book.com/), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/), [SaaS landing-page references](https://saaspo.com/), [MaxiBestOf](https://maxibestof.one/), and original product sites can supply design candidates. A pool is not an endorsement of all its entries. Use same-purpose screens and distinguish archival screenshots from live interfaces. Access, pricing and availability can change; no subscription or live browsing integration is included.

For writing, prefer original matched variants or authorized excerpts. For research, use actual papers and author-provided pages, preserving citations and separating problem preference from evidence quality. See `sources.json` for machine-readable pointers to the references used while forming the workflow.
